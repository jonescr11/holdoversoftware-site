Privacy Policy

Effective: May 2026

App

Holdover is a ballistics calculator developed by CM Collective LLC, a Minnesota limited liability company. holdoversoftware.com is owned and operated by CM Collective LLC.

Data Collection

Holdover does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All ballistic profiles, settings, and calculations are stored locally on your device.

Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services. No usage data, device identifiers, or behavioral information is collected or shared with any third party.

Network Usage

Holdover does not require an internet connection to function. The app performs all calculations on-device and does not communicate with external servers during normal use.

Website

The Holdover app collects nothing, as described above. This website is separate: if you email us or, in the future, submit your email address through a signup or beta-interest form on this site, CM Collective LLC receives the information you choose to provide (such as your email address, along with the date of submission and general region) solely to respond to you or to notify you about Holdover. We do not sell it or use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing hello@holdoversoftware.com.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your information to third parties. The app collects no data to share; any information you submit through this website is used only to respond to you or notify you as described above.

Children's Privacy

Holdover is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact CM Collective LLC at hello@holdoversoftware.com.
