A Solver You Can Trust

A full point-mass solver: G1 and G7 drag, wind, spin drift and Coriolis. Cross-checked against an independently written second solver, agreeing to within 0.02 MOA at 1,000 yards.

Guided Zeroing

Tap your shots on the target and get exact turret corrections: direction, clicks, and MOA. Every confirmed zero is saved, so you always know when and how you last zeroed.

Nearly 900 Factory Loads

Federal, Hornady, Berger, Nosler, Barnes, Lapua and 20+ more brands across 70 calibers, with lab-measured BCs flagged so you know where your numbers come from.

Enter Any Load by Hand

Shooting something the catalog does not list, or rolling your own? Enter it yourself: bullet weight, BC, muzzle velocity, and the powder, charge and OAL behind it.

DOPE Card, Modernized

Pick your distances, choose clean or detailed, and share a print-ready card built from your rifle, your load and your zero.

Set Up in Under a Minute

Mostly tapping, not typing. A short walkthrough on first run, help on every field that needs it, and quick actions on the home screen that jump straight to your holds or your zero.
