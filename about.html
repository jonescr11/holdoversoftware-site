About

The company behind Holdover.

The App

Holdover is a precision ballistics calculator for iOS — built for sport shooters, hunters, and long-range competitors. It produces sub-MOA drop solutions using G1 and G7 drag models, walks shooters through guided zeroing on a tap-to-place target, and ships with a curated library of factory loads with verified ballistic coefficients. Every calculation runs locally on the device. No cell service required.

Principles

Holdover is built around five principles that shape every interaction in the app — from the 60-second first-profile flow to the high-contrast, sunlight-readable drop tables.

  • Shoot first, configure later. Get to a useful drop table in under a minute.
  • Never betray trust. No crashes, no data loss, no silently wrong numbers.
  • The range is the context. Big targets, high contrast, sunlight-readable, one-handed.
  • Progressive disclosure. Casual shooters see simple. Power users unlock advanced.
  • Offline-first. Everything works without cell service.
