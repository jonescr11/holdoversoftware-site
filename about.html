About

The studio, and the company behind it.

The Studio

Holdover Software is a one-person independent app studio. It builds small, focused apps for iOS: each one does a single job properly, runs its work on the device rather than on a server, keeps whatever it saves local to the phone, and asks nobody to make an account.

The studio is named after its first app, and the name stuck as the umbrella when the second one turned out to be for an entirely different audience.

The Apps

For sport shooters and hunters

Holdover Ballistics

A precision ballistics calculator for iOS, built for sport shooters, hunters, and long-range competitors. It produces drop solutions using G1 and G7 drag models, walks shooters through guided zeroing on a tap-to-place target, and ships with a curated library of factory loads with verified ballistic coefficients. Every calculation runs locally on the device. No cell service required.

For kids ages 3 to 8

Poplings

A calm balloon toy for small children. Balloons drift up, a tap pops them with a cheerful sound, and now and then one has a hand-drawn Popling hiding inside. Three worlds, 37 Poplings, and a Grown-Ups section that gives a parent a plain read on how the app is actually being used. No ads, no accounts, no purchases, and nothing collected.

How we build

Two apps this far apart do not share a feature list, but they do share a standard. These are the rules that apply either way.

  • The work happens on the device. Solvers, saved data, and settings live on the phone. Nothing needs a signal.
  • Nothing is collected. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising code in anything we ship. There is no data pipeline behind these apps because we never built one.
  • Never betray trust. No crashes, no data loss, and no silently wrong numbers.
  • The real setting is the design brief. A drop table gets read in direct sun, one-handed, with a heartbeat up. A kids app gets used by someone who cannot read yet. Neither gets designed for a demo.
  • Progressive disclosure. The simple path is the default; depth is there when it is wanted, and never in the way before that.

Ballistics-specific principles

Holdover Ballistics adds one more: shoot first, configure later. A useful drop table in under a minute, before any of the advanced inputs are touched.

Kids-specific principles

Poplings adds two. Toy, not arcade: the default mode is calm and endless, with no timer, no score, and no way to lose. And never gamify screen time: the parent-facing numbers are there to inform a parent, not to reward more play, so there are no streaks and no rewards for coming back.
