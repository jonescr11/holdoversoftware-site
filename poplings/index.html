What’s inside

Four things to do, and one of them is just for grown-ups.

Play

The calm one

Balloons float up at whatever pace suits. Tap one and it pops with a cheerful little chime. Nothing counts down, nothing keeps score, and nothing ever says you did it wrong.

Every button is big enough for a three-year-old to hit on the first try, and the app never asks a child to read anything to get going.

Surprises

Someone hiding inside

Now and then a balloon has a little creature tucked inside it. Those are the Poplings. Each one gets its own sound, its own name, and a spot in My Poplings.

There are 37 in all, and a crayon cat looks crayoned while a clay bear looks squeezed. Toot, the mascot, turns up in every world. Finding them is the only collecting the app asks for, and it is never behind a purchase.

My Poplings

A page to fill up

Every Popling found gets pressed into the book. The empty spots stay visible, which is the whole appeal: a small child can see exactly how much there is left to find.

Tapping one plays its sound again. It works as a quiet activity all by itself.

Pop Party

For when they want a challenge

Older kids usually want something with a bit of a push, so Pop Party is a short timed run with a score. It is the only place in the app where a round can end.

It sits behind its own button and never interrupts calm play, so a younger sibling handed the same phone will not stumble into it.

Grown-Ups

Behind a simple gate

Tucked behind a bit of arithmetic a small child cannot do, you get a plain read on how the app is actually being used: time today and this week, typical visit length, and how the time splits between calm play and Pop Party.

You can also set the difficulty for calm Play (Easy, Just right, or Challenge), adjust the volume or switch on a quiet mode, turn vibration and motion down, and set an optional gentle stop after 10 to 30 minutes. None of it is dressed up as a reward, and none of it turns screen time into a streak to keep.

For parents, in plain terms

Here is everything you would otherwise have to dig through a privacy label to find out.

  • Data collectedNone. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no advertising library in the app. Nothing about your child leaves the device, because nothing is gathered in the first place.
  • AccountsNone. No sign-in, no email, no name, no age entry.
  • AdsNone, of any kind, including cross-promotion of our own apps.
  • PriceFree. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription, and nothing locked behind a payment.
  • Links outThe child-facing part of the app has no links out to the web or to other apps.
  • InternetNot required and not used during play. The app works in airplane mode.
  • Saved on deviceWhich Poplings have been found, your settings, and play-time totals. All of it stays on the phone and is deleted with the app.
