How we build

The same three rules apply whether the user is a competitor at 800 yards or a three-year-old.

On your device

Calculations and saved data stay on the phone. No accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to lose when the signal drops.

Nothing collected

No third-party analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs in any app we ship. There is no data pipeline behind these apps because we did not build one.

Built for the real setting

A drop table gets read in direct sun, one-handed. A kids app gets used by someone who cannot read yet. Both get designed for that, not for a demo.
